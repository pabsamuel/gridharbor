# Privacy — GridHarbor 0.1.0, 23 October 2026

GridHarbor runs locally. It captures visible text only after a toolbar or capture action. chrome.storage.local retains captures, view preferences, recipes and an optional signed license. This data is not synced or separately encrypted by the app. No captured content, browsing history or license is sent to a server. There are no analytics, remote scripts, API calls or account requirements.

activeTab temporarily grants access to the chosen source tab. scripting injects the packaged collector on that page. storage saves the workspace. There are no permanent all-site permissions. Controls and hidden content are omitted; email/mailto/tel detections reject contact data. Capture only non-contact content you are entitled to process. Safeguards do not identify every type of personal information.

Use Delete local data to remove captures, recipes, view state and the saved key. Removing the extension/profile also removes its storage. Exported files and copies on disk must be deleted separately. Original web pages are unchanged. Backups contain captures, not your license key.

Purchases are not active. Any future user-initiated Paddle website checkout is separate and governed by its displayed terms; the extension receives no payment-card data. Future manual activation uses a locally verified signed key.
